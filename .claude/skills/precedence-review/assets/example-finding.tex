\finding[Smallest-eigenvalue comparison for a deformed Hessian]{A Gaussian comparison bound for the smallest eigenvalue of a deformed TAP Hessian}{example-comparison}

\paragraph{Summary.}
The bound on the smallest eigenvalue of the deformed Hessian in
\ploc{Lemma~4.3 (p.~21)}{stability.tex:410--455} follows the Gaussian comparison
argument of Author and Author \sloc{AuthorAuthor2018-Example}{Lemma~3.2,
p.~14}, and the preprint does not cite that paper anywhere. The preprint
presents the bound without attribution; its only nearby citation, to the
semicircle law, covers the limiting spectrum and not the comparison step.
\rel{same technique}: the comparison process, the choice of interpolation and
the resulting variance inequality are the same, applied to a Hessian with an
added diagonal.

\paragraph{Setup.}
$H_D = W + D$, where $W$ is the coupling matrix of Section~\ref{sec:overview}
and $D$ is a diagonal matrix with entries in $[-\delta,\delta]$; $\lambda_{\min}(H_D)$
is its smallest eigenvalue.

\paragraph{Role in the argument.}
Lemma~4.3 gives the positivity input to the Hessian-failure count:
\ploc{Lemma~4.3}{stability.tex:410} $\to$ \ploc{Proposition~5.1}{stability.tex:600}
$\to$ \ploc{Theorem~1.1}{introduction.tex:33}. The same bound is used again in
\ploc{\S6.2}{residuals.tex:120}.

\paragraph{The earlier technique.}
For a symmetric Gaussian matrix $W$ and a deterministic symmetric $A$,
\sloc{AuthorAuthor2018-Example}{Lemma~3.2, p.~14} compares
$\min_{\|x\|=1}\ip{x}{(W+A)x}$ with a scalar Gaussian process through
Sudakov--Fernique, obtaining
$\E\lambda_{\min}(W+A)\ge \lambda_{\min}(A)-2+o(1)$.

\paragraph{Differences.}
\begin{itemize}
\item The deformation is diagonal and depends on the observation, so the
preprint conditions on it before comparing.
\item Concentration of $\lambda_{\min}$ is added through Gaussian
Lipschitz concentration, which the earlier lemma does not state.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{How to check.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \ploc{(4.9)}{stability.tex:430} against the comparison process in the proof
of \sloc{AuthorAuthor2018-Example}{Lemma~3.2, p.~15}.
\item \ploc{(4.11)}{stability.tex:450} against the statement of
\sloc{AuthorAuthor2018-Example}{Lemma~3.2, p.~14} with $A=D$.
\end{enumerate}
